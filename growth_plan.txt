MARVIRA — GROWTH PLAN (USERS FIRST)
================================================================================

Companion: monetization_todo.txt | analytics_requirement.txt | go_live_update_checklist.txt

Legend:
  [ ] = Not started

================================================================================
PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

  Users first. Monetization later.
  Empty cities feel broken; a few packed cities feel magical.
  Do NOT turn on ads or Plus at public launch (v1.0).
  Flip monetization only after retention and hunt density look healthy.

================================================================================
NORTH STAR METRICS (first 3–6 months)
================================================================================

  Primary
    - Weekly active players who finish ≥1 hunt
    - Published hunts in focus city
    - Invite → install → first unlock conversion
    - D7 retention of players who completed a hunt once

  Secondary
    - Organizers who publish ≥1 hunt
    - Share / invite link opens
    - App Store / Play ratings (target after first completion)

  Ignore early: download vanity, ad revenue, Plus conversion

================================================================================
POSITIONING
================================================================================

  Players:     “Turn your city into a scavenger hunt with friends.”
  Organizers:  “Build a GPS hunt and invite with one link — events, schools, tourism.”

  Lead consumer brand with players + weekend energy.
  Organizers scale supply; don’t lead with “enterprise event software.”

    - M2 Plus / IAP

  v1.3 — MARVIRA PLUS + PUBLISH CAP
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Gate: Ads stable ≥2–4 weeks; no retention cliff from v1.2.

  Monetization ON
    [ ] M2.1–M2.5 Plus IAP ($3.99/mo or $29.99/yr) + entitlements + paywall

    - v1.0–v1.2: unlimited create + publish
    - v1.3+: Free = max 10 concurrent PUBLISHED; drafts always unlimited
    - Over-cap users: existing hunts stay live; NEW publish blocked until
      they unpublish down to ≤10 or go Plus (see monetization_todo.txt M3)

  Still OFF / later
    - M1.6 Rewarded cosmetics (optional same release if low risk)
    - M1.7 Rare interstitial
    - B2B web billing
    - Price A/B ($4.99 / $39.99)

  Growth focus: Soft paywall only at Profile / publish limit / remove ads —
                never block starting a hunt; never block creating drafts

  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  v1.4 — ADS DEPTH + OPTIMIZATION
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Monetization ON
    [ ] M1.6 Rewarded cosmetics (frame / share theme — never hints)
    [ ] M1.7 Interstitial after hunt OR practice session (cooldown ≥3–5 min)
    [ ] M4.3 A/B pricing later ($3.99 vs $4.99; yearly $29.99 vs $39.99)
    [ ] M4.4 Store guideline re-check

  Growth focus: Second city expansion + light geo-fenced paid UA

  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
