B3) Data sharing — third parties (short text you can reuse)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  We share limited data with service providers that help us operate the app:
  - Google Firebase Analytics — product usage events and identifiers
  - Google Firebase Crashlytics — crash and diagnostic data
  - Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — device push tokens for notifications
  - Our hosting / API infrastructure — account, gameplay, location checks,
    and in-app notification inbox

  We do not sell user data.
  We do not use data for third-party advertising in v1.0.
