Age / maturity (expected answers for Marvira v1.0)

  • Gambling / Contests: No paid gambling. Competitive leaderboards / optional gift codes
    for first finishers — declare “Contests” carefully if gift codes are real prizes.
  • Mature / Suggestive: None intended
  • Violence: None

  • Alcohol / Tobacco / Drugs: None intended
  • Medical / Treatment: No

Google Play content rating (IARC):
  Complete the questionnaire honestly.

  Users can create content (hunts / questions) → declare User-Generated Content if asked.
