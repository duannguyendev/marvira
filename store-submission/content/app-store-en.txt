Description (max 4000)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Turn your city into a scavenger hunt.

Marvira is a location-based adventure app. Walk to real places, unlock stops with GPS, answer challenges, and compete on leaderboards — alone or with friends.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Find a hunt — browse events near you or open an invite link.
2. Walk to places — follow the map to real landmarks. GPS checks you in when you arrive.
3. Answer challenges — quizzes and clues at each stop. Discovery is the point.
4. Climb the leaderboard — earn points for speed and accuracy, then share your finish.

CREATE YOUR OWN HUNTS
Design GPS scavenger hunts for teams, birthdays, tourism, campuses, and schools. Publish publicly or protect with a password, then invite players with one link.

FEATURES
• Nearby scavenger hunts and city challenges
• GPS place unlock for fair, on-location play
• Multiple question types (text, true/false, multiple choice, image)
• Event and global leaderboards
• Favorites, practice questions, and in-app feedback
• Create and manage your own hunts (My Events)
• Languages: English, Vietnamese, Chinese, Japanese
• Sign in with email, Google, Apple, or Facebook

WHO IT’S FOR
Friends exploring a city, teams bonding outdoors, tourism routes, schools, and anyone who wants adventure on foot.

PRIVACY & SAFETY
Marvira is not directed at children under 13. Precise location is used for gameplay unlock. See our Privacy Policy for details.
